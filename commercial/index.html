Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Oshkosh HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the stuffy dining room right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
