Two common situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can prevent a much larger repair.

How Racine Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
